Andrew Duchon is a poet and programmer exploring what happens when language is
pushed through successive acts of translation — from Elizabethan English to
modern free verse, from verse to image, from image to sculpture.

*Transbliterations, Volume Two: Shakespeare* rewrites all 154 of Shakespeare's
sonnets and grows each one into a garden of AI-generated sculpture.
